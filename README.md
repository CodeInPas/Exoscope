**EXO-SCOPE** is a highly immersive, retro-futuristic exoplanet exploration simulator. Acting as a deep-space telemetry operator, you are tasked with discovering habitable worlds, decoding mysterious alien transmissions, and surviving the perils of the cosmos—all through a Command Line Interface (CLI) and an atmospheric, data-driven HUD.

Rather than relying on 3D graphics, EXO-SCOPE builds tension through abstract data analysis, time-sensitive emergencies, and authentic sound design.

### 🚀 Key Features
* **CLI-Driven Gameplay:** Connect to sectors, apply noise filters, and deploy surface probes entirely through terminal commands.
* **Retro-Futuristic Aesthetic:** Authentic CRT monitor effects, scanlines, and vignette rendering powered by BGRABitmap.
* **Atmospheric Sound Design:** Fully integrated BASS audio featuring mechanical radar sweeps, fast-paced typewriter terminal feedback, and chilling ambient space noise.
* **Interactive Starmap:** Explore a massive 3000x3000 procedurally generated galaxy with telescope panning and gravitational lensing distortions near Black Holes.
* **High-Stakes Decryption:** Intercept and decrypt anomalous signals (biosignatures or megastructures) under extreme time pressure.
* **Economy & Progression:** Save/Load your progress, manage your research funding, upgrade your sensors, and avoid heavy penalties from catastrophic failures.

### 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Language/IDE:** Free Pascal / Lazarus
* **Graphics:** BGRABitmap (Custom UI and rendering engine)
* **Audio:** BASS Audio Library
